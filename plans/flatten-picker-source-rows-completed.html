Replace synthetic source sections with a stable inline source column. Filtering changes the result set, not the shape of the list.

Brief

Remove the outermost picker type sections such as open and root. Show the type on every result row in a dedicated left column, retain each source’s current color, and keep the Open workspace/tab topology inside the row.

02

Layout contract

Source column

  • Render Entry::source_name() on every selectable row.
  • Use the existing source_color(theme, source) mapping.
  • Calculate width from all loaded entries, not app.filtered, so query changes cannot move later columns.
  • Cap label content at 10 terminal cells. Measure and truncate with ratatui’s Span::width(), then pad every row to the stable source-column width without adding a dependency.

Row content

  • Remove source banners and the non-Open ├─/└─ branches they implied.
  • Repeat open on workspace and tab rows.
  • In detailed mode, derive the topology-prefix budget from every loaded Open entry and its ancestry depth, independent of query, source filter, or expansion state.
  • Derive the right-side status budget from all loaded entries instead of filtered rows. Together with the source and topology budgets, this keeps title and path columns fixed while filtering.
  • Keep Open expansion, tab ancestry, pin/focus glyphs, worktree marker, title, path, metadata, score, and selection styling.
  • Keep detailed and compact modes available under the existing configuration.

Scope boundary

This is a rendering change. Filtering, fuzzy ranking, source shortcuts, source ordering, actions, preview content, refresh behavior, and exit codes keep their current contracts.

07

Risks and mitigations

Fixed source, topology, and status columns reduce title/path space
Medium
Cap source content at 10 terminal cells and reuse saturating width budgets plus end truncation at 42–50 columns.
Open topology loses readable ancestry
Medium
Remove only the outer source tree. Keep workspace expansion and tab ancestry glyphs inside a query-independent prefix budget.
Selection or mouse rows drift after header removal
Medium
Assert a direct one-to-one mapping between rendered items and app.filtered, including scrolled mouse hits.
Source identity loses its visual cue
Low
Apply the existing source-specific color to every inline label and test representative source cells.

Owner decisions

None remain open. Inline labels repeat on every row, source colors stay unchanged, ranking and interaction behavior remain outside the change.
